Kana Trainer

Reference tool · 5 minutes a day · Remembers your leeches between sessions

Typed recall, timed. Under 1.8 seconds counts as solid; slower stays in rotation. Miss a kana with a lookalike twin and the twin is queued a couple of cards later, so you have to tell them apart rather than get lucky. Progress is stored in this browser — bookmark this page and come back to the same one.

Running Full 92? Do it first, cold. Not after a few drills — first thing, before anything else, ideally on a different day from your last session. A warmed-up 92 measures your warm-up. Both re-measurements so far were run straight after a drilling session, which is why neither can be compared to the baseline.

How the three modes differ

ModePicks fromUse it when
Daily drillAll 92, weighted — leeches ~20× more often than mastered onesEvery day. This is the default.
Confusables onlyJust the lookalike pairs you haven't masteredWhen the drill keeps surfacing the same twins.
Full 92Everything once, shuffled, minimal feedbackMonthly, cold, to re-measure. Don't warm up first.
Scoring, so you can trust it: a kana is mastered after three consecutive fast-and-correct answers. Correct-but-slow resets you to one — it's still knowledge you're reconstructing. Any miss resets to zero and adds a lifetime lapse; two lapses marks it a leech and it starts appearing constantly until it's clean.
